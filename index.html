01 / About

Strong types. Small changes. Systems that last.

I settled on Haskell because it lets me refactor with confidence. I favour clear types, property-based tests, and changes that are small enough to review well. I enjoy taking a feature from the first design conversation through implementation, tests, documentation, and release.

Since 2015, I’ve worked with remote and asynchronous teams. I value thoughtful review, honest tests, and sharing what I learn.
